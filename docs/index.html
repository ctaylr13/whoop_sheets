Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WHOOP to Google Sheets (the “Integration”) handles information when you connect your WHOOP account. The Integration accesses WHOOP information only after you grant permission through WHOOP’s authorization process.

Information the Integration accesses

The Integration requests permission to read sleep and recovery information from WHOOP and to maintain access using an OAuth refresh token. Depending on what WHOOP makes available for those permissions, this information may include sleep and cycle identifiers, sleep start and end times, recovery score, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep performance, sleep consistency, sleep efficiency, respiratory rate, sleep-stage durations, sleep need, blood-oxygen saturation, skin temperature, and WHOOP record timestamps.

How information is used

The Integration uses WHOOP information only to place the account owner’s sleep and recovery metrics into their own Google Sheet and to update matching daily check-in rows. WHOOP information is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or used to train machine-learning or artificial-intelligence models.

Storage and retention

Synced WHOOP information is stored in the Google Sheet selected by the account owner. The WHOOP client credentials are stored in Google Apps Script Properties, and the account owner’s OAuth tokens are stored in their Apps Script user properties. Google processes this information as necessary to operate Apps Script and Google Sheets.

Synced information remains in the Google Sheet until the account owner deletes it. Authorization information remains in Apps Script until the account owner resets or removes the authorization. Information is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Sharing

WHOOP information is not shared with or sold to advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties. It is processed by WHOOP and Google only as necessary to authorize the connection, retrieve the requested information, run the Integration, and store the results in the account owner’s Google Sheet. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

Your choices and deletion

The account owner can stop future access by using the Integration’s “Reset WHOOP authorization” command and by revoking the Integration in WHOOP. Synced information can be deleted directly from the Google Sheet. The account owner can also remove the Apps Script project and its stored properties.

For help accessing or deleting information handled by the Integration, contact ctaylr13@gmail.com.

Children’s privacy

The Integration is not directed to children under 13, and its developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the Integration changes. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
